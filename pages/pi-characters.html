Profile

Kaia Vaelor is the Pneuma Institute's Chief Diplomatic Officer — the face the Institute presents to the outside world when it chooses to present one at all. She is female, Avriethrax, twenty-four years old, and by any objective measure one of the most psionically gifted individuals alive. Her abdominal scales carry the standard purple-green hue of mature Avriethrax, her feathers a blend of white and grey across her back, wings, crest and tail-tip. Her most immediately striking feature is her eyes — all four are white. Complete blindness, a rare genetic disorder affecting only a few thousand Avriethrax in recorded history.

She does not experience this as a disadvantage. Not anymore.

Psionic Ability — Echolocation

Kaia is a powerful psion by any standard — and her blindness appears to be the cause rather than a coincidence. Most congenitally blind Avriethrax develop compensatory psionic sensitivity; Kaia developed something considerably beyond that. She perceives her environment through a form of psionic echolocation: a periodic low chirp, barely noticeable in conversation, that she amplifies psionically to paint a vivid spatial model of her surroundings directly within her mind. Effective range unaided: approximately 500km. With her experimental resonance harness, worn constantly, this range extends to near-planetary scale.

The limitations are real and she is aware of them. She perceives the world in high-resolution monochrome — no colour, ever. Fast-moving objects are difficult to track unless she is chirping continuously or they are producing sound. In silence, anything moving quietly can vanish from her perception entirely. She has adapted her environment and her habits accordingly, and after twenty-four years the adaptation is seamless enough that most people who work with her for the first time do not realise she is blind until someone tells them.

Personality

Kaia presents as warm, curious, and openly playful — qualities that read as disarming in the context of the Institute, which produces few individuals with anything like a natural social register. She is genuinely fascinated by other species, by the differences in cognition and culture that make cross-species diplomacy the genuinely complex problem it is, and her curiosity is not performed. In conversation she is attentive in the way only someone who cannot rely on visual cues learns to be — she hears everything.

The playfulness is real. The seriousness underneath it is equally real. She has spent her career in an institution that runs on secrecy and classified information, and she has learned to be as careful with what she does not say as with what she does. In formal negotiations she shifts into a mode that her counterparts consistently describe as unsettling — precise, unhurried, apparently aware of things she should not be. Whether this is exceptional preparation, psionic perception, or something she absorbed from proximity to Seraph Prime before she hatched, no one has been able to determine.

Background — The Egg Incident

Kaia's origin is one of the more unusual on record within the Institute, and the full details are classified above her own clearance level. The available version is this: her egg was accidentally displaced during a routine maintenance cycle in the Institute's deep-level infrastructure, rolling into an air duct and falling several levels before coming to rest in proximity to the Cruciform Vault — the sealed chamber 26km below the surface that contains the collapsed remains of Seraph Prime.

Standard protocol for any biological material exposed to the Vault's psionic pressure field is immediate quarantine and almost certain loss. Eggs do not survive. The psionic pressure alone is sufficient to cause embryonic collapse. Kaia survived. She hatched early, was found by a maintenance team who did not immediately understand what they had found, and was brought to the Institute's medical division with no expectation that she would be neurologically intact.

She was intact. She was blind. Her psionic architecture was, by every measure the Institute had, profoundly altered. The working theory — unconfirmed, classified at Archon level — is that some fragment of Seraph Prime's residual essence passed into the metaphysical substrate that Avriethrax draw their psionic ability from, permanently amplifying what would have been a standard psionic profile into something the Institute has not encountered before or since. The blindness is understood to be a side effect of the same event — the visual architecture simply did not survive the pressure. Everything else was restructured rather than destroyed.

Role within the Institute

Kaia's appointment as CDO is, on the surface, an unusual choice. She is young, her background is anomalous, and her public profile — warm, personable, visibly blind — does not match the Institute's default presentation of itself. This is precisely why she was chosen. The Institute requires a face for external dealings that does not immediately communicate what the Institute actually is. Kaia provides that face with complete authenticity, because her warmth and curiosity are genuine.

What external parties consistently fail to account for is that she is also, at all times, operating at a psionic sensitivity level that gives her a read on the emotional state of every individual in a room. She does not use this intrusively — she is not a telepath in the classical sense — but she perceives stress, deception, and hesitation as clearly as most people read facial expressions. In negotiations, she knows when a counterpart is lying before they have finished the sentence.
