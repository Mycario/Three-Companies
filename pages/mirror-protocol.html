Overview

Mirror Protocol is not a company in the conventional sense. It does not maintain a public headquarters, does not publish a personnel count, and does not formally acknowledge most of its operations. What is known is pieced together from its clients, its occasional public-facing contracts, and the quiet fear it commands in intelligence circles across Apostolos.

The organisation was founded by a Noctivex whose name has never been officially recorded — a deliberate choice. What began as a Noctivex rights movement, pushing back against the Institute's engineered subjugation of the species, gradually evolved into something altogether different. Mirror Protocol concluded that the most effective way to protect Noctivex interests was not protest or political pressure, but leverage. Information is leverage. Infiltration is leverage. The ability to vanish and reappear anywhere, undetected, is the most profound leverage of all.

Today, Mirror Protocol operates as an intelligence contractor and infiltration specialist, offering its services to clients who can meet its price and its standards. The Institute is among those clients, though the relationship is one of mutual wariness rather than alignment. Mirror Protocol does not answer to the Archons, does not submit to PICS oversight, and has never been successfully surveilled. For an organisation built on the premise that nothing stays hidden, this is a point of considerable institutional pride.

The Founder still leads. How long they have led, and how they have remained effective across the decades since the organisation's founding, is not publicly known.

Divisions
Intelligence
Information collection, analysis, and brokering. The organisation's core capability and primary revenue source.
Field Operations
Infiltration, extraction, and direct action. Operates exclusively via Noctivex agents and stealth-rated assets.
Stealth R&D
Development of proprietary stealth technology. Produces the MPC suit line and the Vaethos-Nul infiltrator platform.
External Relations
Manages client contracts and inter-company dealings. The only division with a public-facing presence.
